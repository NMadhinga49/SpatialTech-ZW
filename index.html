About SpatialTech Zimbabwe

Built on engineering precision and accessible spatial education

Founded in Harare by Nettey Madhinga—a B.Sc. (Hons) Geomatics and Surveying Engineering graduate from Gwanda State University and ZIMDEF prize recipient—SpatialTech Zimbabwe bridges classical land surveying rigor with modern open-source GIS and digital tools.

From assisting local infrastructure and cadastral workflows to mentoring the next generation of GIS practitioners across Zimbabwe and beyond, our focus is simple: deliver clear, accurate, and dependable work every single time.
